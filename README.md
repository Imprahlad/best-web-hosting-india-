## How to Choose Web Hosting in India (2026) {#how-to-choose}

These are the factors that actually determine hosting quality — not what the marketing pages say.

### 1. Server Location and TTFB

If your audience is primarily in India, choose a host with a **Mumbai or Delhi data center**. Our tests showed a 35–60% TTFB reduction compared to US-based servers without CDN. With Cloudflare properly configured, the gap narrows to 10–20%, making international hosts viable.

### 2. Introductory Price vs Renewal Price

Most hosts advertise deep introductory discounts. Renewal increases ranged from 180% (Bluehost India) to 305% (GreenGeeks) in our comparison. Always check the renewal rate before purchasing — a ₹59/month plan that renews at ₹200/month changes your 3-year total cost significantly.

### 3. Shared vs VPS vs Cloud — Which Do You Need?

| Traffic / Use Case | Recommended Type | Typical Price Range |
|---|---|---|
| New blog, <500 visits/day | Shared hosting | ₹59–₹250/mo |
| Growing site, 500–5,000 visits/day | Managed VPS | ₹1,000–₹2,500/mo |
| eCommerce, 5,000+ visits/day | Cloud hosting (Cloudways) | ₹1,175/mo+ |
| Custom stack / enterprise | Dedicated or bare metal | ₹8,000+/mo |

### 4. PHP Version and Caching Stack

In 2026, WordPress performance depends heavily on **PHP 8.2+** and **LiteSpeed/LSCache** or Redis object caching. Avoid hosts still running PHP 7.x or Apache-only environments without a clear upgrade path.

### 5. INR Billing and Indian Payment Methods

Hosting billed in USD carries 18% GST plus forex conversion costs for Indian buyers. Hosts with INR billing (YouStable, BigRock, Bluehost India) reduce administrative overhead and allow UPI/Paytm/net banking. Factor the full cost — including GST — into your 12-month budget.

### What is TTFB and why does it matter?

TTFB (Time to First Byte) is the time between a browser sending a request and receiving the first byte from the server. Google's Core Web Vitals define "good" TTFB as under 800ms. Lower TTFB directly improves LCP (Largest Contentful Paint), a direct Google ranking signal. For Indian websites, a Mumbai data center reduces TTFB by 35–60% vs US-based servers.

### Is shared hosting enough for an Indian eCommerce site?

For stores with under 500 daily visitors and fewer than 50 products, shared hosting handles the load adequately. Beyond that, move to managed VPS (ScalaHosting ~₹1,676/month) or cloud hosting (Cloudways ~₹1,175/month) to handle concurrent transactions and prevent slow checkout pages.
