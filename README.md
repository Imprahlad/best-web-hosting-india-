🧠 How to Choose the Right Web Host in 2025?
When selecting a hosting provider, consider the following:

Performance (Speed + Uptime) – Look for NVMe SSD or LiteSpeed servers.

Support – 24/7 chat or phone support can be a lifesaver.

Location of Data Center – Choose India or nearby if your audience is local.

Freebies – SSL, domain, backups, and email hosting can add significant value.

Scalability – Ensure the host lets you upgrade as your site grows.
